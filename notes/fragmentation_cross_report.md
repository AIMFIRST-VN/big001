# Second referee (agent imbh-cross, 2026-09-04): verdicts on the attack report (A) and the draft model (B)

1. Hubble condition vs clump: REFEREE RIGHT, stronger in fall-back: a ball 1e40 inside r_s has no sub-Hubble exterior; sub-clumps sit inside one common trapped region, no own apparent horizon or clock. PBH formation needs an untrapped (expanding) surrounding, i.e. the kernel already expanding after the bounce = standard PBH formation; fall-back does no work. Worse: a jammed hard-sphere medium cannot carry density contrast above 0.74/0.64-1 = 0.16 (crystalline vs random close packing), so sigma(M) <= 0.16 < delta_c at every scale; beta_max = 4e-3 per e-fold, f = 0.82 (no radiation era). Jammed packings are hyperuniform (sigma^2 ~ M^-4/3). Gravitational fragmentation of a jammed ball does not happen in the paper's own ansatz.
2. Two clocks: REFEREE RIGHT on inconsistency; BOTH WRONG on the clock. Haggard-Rovelli (M/m_P)^2 t_P is time at infinity for a shell with an exterior. Bianchi, Christodoulou, D'Ambrosio, Haggard & Rovelli 2018: tunnelling suppressed by exp(-M^2/m_P^2); hole Hawking-evaporates ~M^3 t_P then tunnels into a long-lived Planck-mass white-hole REMNANT (lifetime ~M^4), proposed as dark matter (Rovelli & Vidotto 2018). That remnant IS the paper's relic; Sec 5 already uses N^3 t_P. With the Hawking clock: pre-BBN M < 2e5 kg, pre-recombination < 5e9 kg, permanent above ~2e11 kg (not 6e22). All toy windows mislabelled.
3. Explosion spectrum: REFEREE RIGHT. Haggard-Rovelli is time-symmetric: what fell in comes out. Barrau, Bolliet, Vidotto & Weimer 2016: low-energy component at hole size (8 MeV at 1e14 kg, 80 keV at 1e16, 8 keV at 1e17, 3 eV at 3e20) plus high-energy component at the collapsed matter's temperature. For relic-built holes the returned matter is relics; only the Hawking phase converts mass, leaving a remnant. "Conversion is complete" is false; the note reduces to Sec 5's merge-then-evaporate = 4(a).
4. BBN numbers: REFEREE WRONG both ways. delta rho/rho_rad = 1.33 m_X Y_X/T, T = 1.56 g*^-1/4 t^-1/2 MeV. KKMT 2018 (1 TeV) hadronic: 3e-9 (1e2 s), 2e-9 (1e4), 1e-8 (1e6), 1e-7 (1e8), 3e-6 (1e10); EM: none below ~1e5 s, 3e-5 (1e6), 3e-6 (1e8), 1e-5 (1e10). Referee's hadronic entries at 1e2-1e4 s are 2-3 decades too loose. But hadronic bounds do not apply: for M > 1e14 kg hole-scale quanta < 8 MeV, below pion threshold; for M > 1e16 kg below the 2.2 MeV D threshold. Only a Planck-scale Hawking component could hit hadrons. Real constraints in 1e15-1e19 kg: entropy/eta shift between BBN and CMB (few percent) and mu/y after 1e6 s. SERIOUS, not fatal, mis-derived.
5. Astrophysical bounds: REFEREE RIGHT. Rate ~t^-34/37 rises with z generically. CMB accretion f < ~1e-4 at 1e3 Msun, <= 1e-5 at 1e4 (Serpico 2020, Poulin 2017); dwarf heating 1e-3..1e-2 at 1e3-1e4 (Brandt 2016; Koushiappas & Loeb 2017); Lyman-alpha fM < 60 Msun (Murgia 2019). "One per dwarf" excluded by Eri II / Segue 1.
6. Horizon-mass identification: MODEL ARTEFACT. M_H = m_P t/t_P is the Hubble mass of an expanding FRW restarted at Planck density; for a re-collapsing bound region the scale is the local free-fall mass = m_P. The toy is a textbook post-bounce PBH mass function with "re-jam" renamed t = 0.
7. Sequential depletion: arithmetic RIGHT (tail = f(e^I - 1)); kill criterion WRONG. Sec 5's f = T_eq/T_conv is all non-relativistic mass to radiation; if holes are permanent, (relics + tail) = T_eq/T_conv and f_PBH = tail/(relics+tail) is unconstrained by the budget. Local DM is the kernel's non-relativistic matter (ejecta relics are not in our galaxies). With the Hawking clock the permanent tail starts at ~2e11 kg, so the asteroid window 1e17-1e23 kg (f_PBH = 1 allowed) is available. The toy required tail/f < 1e-3 at all masses, killing what observation allows.
8. Blue ALIVE window: MODEL ARTEFACT plus tuning. In every ALIVE row sigma(m_P) = 21-1e4: 99% of the mass collapses into holes below 100 m_P in the first Planck times = Sec 5's 93-generation evaporation with the cross-section problem replaced by an assumed sigma >> 1 at the Planck scale, where linear Press-Schechter is meaningless and the hard-sphere cap forbids it. f ~ 1e-14 in every ALIVE row regardless of q and pivot: exp(-N_e), not physics. sigma(M_L) ~ 2e-5 holds only at q = 0.1 (q = 0.15: 2e-7; 0.05: 2e-3): two parameters fitted to one number, n = 1.6 wrong anyway. Coincidence.
9. Other artefacts: injection ratio ignores that radiation redshifts relative to PBH matter during a PBH-dominated era (bound tighter by a_era/a_explode); flat spectrum uses n_s = 0.965 down to m_P with no break; the Planck-star clock is the retracted one. Kolmogorov null right but trivial.
