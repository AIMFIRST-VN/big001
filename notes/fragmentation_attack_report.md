# Referee report on notes/fragmentation_imbh_idea.md (agent imbh-attack, 2026-09-04)

Verdict: two fatal internal inconsistencies, three serious phenomenological conflicts, rest cosmetic.

1. (a) FATAL: "inside its Schwarzschild radius" is the Hubble condition, not fragmentation. At any density rho every sphere of radius R > c/H satisfies 2GM/c^2 > R; at Planck density that radius is l_P and the mass m_P. It holds for every sub-volume of a homogeneous medium and singles out no clump. A sub-clump has no exterior and no asymptotic region, so r_s and the Planck-star external clock t=(M/m_P)^2 t_P are undefined for it. Separate holes require a clump to decouple from the flow after expansion (delta > delta_c ~ 0.45 at horizon entry), i.e. ordinary PBH formation with M ~ M_H(t)=c^3 t/G: 1e14 kg at t=3e-22 s, 1e4 Msun at t=0.05 s (T~4 MeV). Then Planck density and relic hard-sphere physics play no role and the mechanism is a standard PBH scenario needing O(0.1) curvature perturbations, which the paper's spectrum (n_s fitted, A_s=2e-9) does not supply.

2. (h) FATAL: two clocks for one object. The kernel (M~1e53 kg) is itself 1e40 inside r_s (open problem 2) and the paper rebounds it instantaneously via the LQC equation. The Planck-star clock applied to the kernel gives 1e78 s. Either the LQC clock applies to all jammed regions (sub-clumps rebound with the ball; no PBHs, no conversion) or the Planck-star clock does (kernel never bounced). Also a Haggard-Rovelli white hole returns the collapsed matter: if relics survive Planck-density jamming (founding premise), a Planck-star fragment spits out relics, not radiation; "conversion is complete" contradicts Sec 2. Rescue: an argument that trapped-surface dynamics destroys relics, but then it should already have happened in the first ball and 4(a) would not exist.

3. (b) SERIOUS: energy injection budget. Products (8 MeV at 1e14 kg, 8 keV at 1e17 kg, 3 eV at 3e20 kg) thermalise fine before ~1e10 s. Kawasaki-Kohri-Moroi bounds converted to rho_frag/rho_rad at explosion: tau 1e2 s (9e14 kg): <1e-6 hadronic, 1e-2 EM; tau 1e4-1e8 s (1e16-1e18 kg): <4e-8 to 1e-7 hadronic, 4e-5 to 1e-3 EM; tau 1e10 s (1e19 kg): <4e-6 hadronic, 1e-5 EM; then FIRAS mu<9e-5, y<1.5e-5 to recombination. Planck stars explode into all species so the hadronic bound applies: mass fraction in 1e16-1e19 kg must be <1e-7 while the bulk (<1e14 kg) carries ~1: a fall of 7 decades in 4 decades of mass. Continued to 2e34 kg gives 1e-35, whereas an IMBH tail at f_PBH=1e-3 needs 1e-31 (f_surv=1e-28) to 1e-15. A single monotonic mass function cannot both pass BBN and populate IMBHs. Rescue: bimodal with a gap 1e14-1e22 kg, unmotivated.

4. (c) SERIOUS: no characteristic mass; the only one at Planck density is m_P. "f = mass that did not fragment" in a medium unstable at all scales is undefined; with complete conversion T_conv -> T_P, f=1e-28 is still imposed by hand. Replaces one free number with a free function.

5. (d) SERIOUS: quoted bounds optimistic by 1-2 decades at 1e3-1e5 Msun. CMB accretion (Poulin et al. 2017; Serpico et al. 2020): f<~1e-3 at 1e2 Msun, ~1e-4 at 1e3, <1e-5 by 1e4 (disk accretion tighter). Ultra-faint dwarf heating (Brandt 2016; Koushiappas & Loeb 2017): f<~1e-2 at 1e2, ~1e-3 at 1e3-1e4. Wide binaries f<0.1. X-ray/radio background (Inoue & Kusenko 2017) 1e-3..1e-4 over 1e2-1e5. Lyman-alpha Poisson (Murgia et al. 2019): f M < ~60 Msun. "One per dwarf" near-excluded by Segue-1/Eri II heating.

6. (e) Counts: f=1e-3, M=1e4 Msun: 1e5 per MW halo correct; a dwarf halo (1e9 Msun) gets 100, a globular cluster ~0. LISA rate rising with z is generic to every PBH binary population, not a discriminant. Recoil stall at a few 1e2 Msun correct (Holley-Bockelmann et al. 2008). Eddington: t_Sal=45 Myr; 1e2->1e9 Msun needs 725 Myr vs 770 available at z=7; 1e4->1e9 needs 518 Myr. Say "1e4", not "1e2-1e4".

7. (f) Holes formed before the radiation and uncorrelated with it are white-noise isocurvature; Lyman-alpha binds: passes at fM=10 Msun, fails at 1e5 Msun. The note's "clumps sink to the centre" makes the distribution radially stratified -> a large-scale IMBH density gradient across our horizon, a P12-type prediction the note omits and which Sec 5.4's flatness arguments then bound.

8. (g) "Individual relics free of every lensing constraint" dies once any tail exists; f_PBH(M) must then be stated over 1e22 kg-1e5 Msun.

9. (h) cosmetic: mantle fall-back re-jams late; its fragments explode after the kernel's radiation exists: late, off-centre injection at fixed sign, in tension with P12 saturation.

Bottom line: as written, a relabelling of 4(a) (finding 4), inconsistent with the kernel's own bounce (1-2). Defensible residue: if a PBH tail exists, BBN/mu/y force a gap 1e14-1e22 kg and the IMBH end is bounded at ~1e-5 by CMB accretion: a constraint on the paper, not a prediction of it.
