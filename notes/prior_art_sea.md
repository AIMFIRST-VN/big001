# Prior art: sea, wave and turbulence models in cosmology

For the ocean/pool analogies in `big-pool-bangs.md` (Sec. 2 rogue-wave nucleation, Sec. 5.3 cascade, Sec. 11f Johnson–Mehl cells, epilogue "surf, not swell"). Verdicts: **must / should / nice**. **[A]** = anticipates a claim; **[C]** = contradicts one.

## 1. Random seas → peaks theory

- Rice S. O., 1944/45, "Mathematical analysis of random noise", Bell Syst. Tech. J. 23, 282; 24, 46. **should**
- Longuet-Higgins M. S., 1957, "The statistical analysis of a random, moving surface", Phil. Trans. R. Soc. A 249, 321. Maxima statistics of a random sea; ancestor of peaks theory. **must**
- Doroshkevich A. G., 1970, Astrofizika 6, 581. Gaussian-field shear-tensor statistics for structure. **should**
- Adler R. J., 1981, *The Geometry of Random Fields*, Wiley. **nice**
- Bardeen, Bond, Kaiser, Szalay, 1986, "The statistics of peaks of Gaussian random fields", ApJ 304, 15; explicitly builds on Rice and Longuet-Higgins. **must**

Where: Sec. 2, "the same statistics that make one ocean wave in ten thousand a rogue wave" → add "(Rice 1944; Longuet-Higgins 1957), the statistics underlying peak theory (Doroshkevich 1970; BBKS 1986)".

## 2. The turbulent universe (direct prior art for Sec. 5.3)

- von Weizsäcker C. F., 1951, ApJ 114, 165. **must**
- Gamow G., 1952, "The role of turbulence in the evolution of the universe", Phys. Rev. 86, 251. **must**
- Ozernoy L. M., Chernin A. D., 1968, Sov. Astron. 11, 907 (Astron. Zh. 44, 1131, 1967); 12, 901 (1969). **must**
- Ozernoy & Chibisov, 1971, Sov. Astron. 14, 615. **should**
- Silk J., Ames S., 1972, "Primordial turbulence and the formation of galaxies", ApJ 178, 77. **should**
- Peebles P. J. E., 1971, "Primeval turbulence?", Ap&SS 11, 443; *Large-Scale Structure* (1980) §95. Vorticity decays; turbulence at recombination over-produces anisotropy. **must**
- Jones B. J. T., 1976, Rev. Mod. Phys. 48, 107. Why the whirl theory died. **must**

**[A]** Gamow and Ozernoy–Chernin proposed a turbulent-cascade origin of the spectrum; Sec. 5.3 should say so. **[C]** Turbulence at recombination gives δT/T ≳ 10⁻³ against 10⁻⁵ (Peebles 1971; Jones 1976; COBE). The paper's cascade is pre-conversion, so it survives only if the turbulence thermalizes before last scattering; state this in Sec. 5.3 and Limitation 3.

## 3. Pancakes as wave breaking; Burgers / adhesion

- Zel'dovich Ya. B., 1970, A&A 5, 84. **must**
- Arnold, Shandarin, Zel'dovich, 1982, Geophys. Astrophys. Fluid Dyn. 20, 111. Catastrophe-theory caustics. **should**
- Shandarin & Zel'dovich, 1989, "The large-scale structure of the universe: turbulence, intermittency, structures in a self-gravitating medium", Rev. Mod. Phys. 61, 185. **must**
- Gurbatov, Saichev, Shandarin, 1989, MNRAS 236, 385; review 2012, Phys. Usp. 55, 223. Adhesion model = 3-D Burgers. **must**
- Vergassola, Dubrulle, Frisch, Noullez, 1994, A&A 289, 325. **should**
- Hidding, Shandarin, van de Weygaert, 2014, MNRAS 437, 3442 (arXiv:1311.7134). Caustic skeleton; the pool-floor light-caustic analogy is theirs. **must**

Where: epilogue "the same equation that breaks water on a shore, Burgers'" → cite Zel'dovich 1970; Gurbatov et al. 1989; Shandarin & Zel'dovich 1989; Hidding et al. 2014. Caution: Burgers is the *adhesion* approximation, not the shallow-water breaking equation; say "the same nonlinear-advection equation". **[A]** Shandarin & Zel'dovich 1989 already framed structure formation as turbulence and intermittency.

## 4. Rogue waves, nucleation, bubble collisions

- Dysthe, Krogstad, Müller, 2008, "Oceanic rogue waves", Annu. Rev. Fluid Mech. 40, 287. **should**
- Onorato et al., 2013, Phys. Rep. 528, 47. **should**
- Coleman S., 1977, PRD 15, 2929; Callan & Coleman, 1977, PRD 16, 1762; Coleman & De Luccia 1980 (cited). **must** for Γ ∼ e^{−S}.
- Guth & Weinberg, 1983, "Could the universe have recovered from a slow first-order phase transition?", Nucl. Phys. B 212, 321. Percolation. **must**
- Chang, Kleban, Levi, 2009, "Watching worlds collide", JCAP 04, 025 (arXiv:0810.5128). Collision → azimuthally symmetric disc, cold or hot, plus a power asymmetry along the axis. **must**
- Kleban M., 2011, CQG 28, 204008 (arXiv:1107.2593); Aguirre & Johnson 2011 (cited). **should**
- Feeney, Johnson, Mortlock, Peiris, 2011, PRL 107, 071301 (arXiv:1012.1995); PRD 84, 043507. WMAP7: < 1.6 detectable collisions full-sky. **must**
- Jinno, Konstandin, Rubira, Stomberg, 2022, "Bubble clustering in cosmological first order phase transitions", PRD 105, 043510. Nucleation sites cluster like rare Gaussian peaks. **should**

No prior use of rogue-wave tail statistics for vacuum decay found; the framing is new but re-describes thermal activation. **[A]** Chang–Kleban–Levi 2009 anticipate "neighbouring bang imprints a disc-shaped spot with a power asymmetry" almost verbatim; Sec. 9 must acknowledge. **[C]** Feeney et al. 2011 and Planck 2013 XXIII find no collision discs; the eight spots need reconciling (different profile, or sub-threshold).
