# Prior art: dating the storm from the waves

## 1. Dating and locating storms from ocean swell

- **Munk, W. H. 1947**, "Tracking storms by forerunners of swell", J. Meteorology 4, 45-57. First use of the dispersion clock: low-frequency forerunners arrive first; frequency-vs-arrival-time slope gives distance and origin time. **must**
- **Barber, N. F. & Ursell, F. 1948**, "The generation and propagation of ocean waves and swell. I. Wave periods and velocities", Phil. Trans. R. Soc. A 240, 527-560. Period-ridge method; storms in the Atlantic located and dated from Cornwall. **must**
- **Munk, W. H., Miller, G. R., Snodgrass, F. E. & Barber, N. F. 1963**, "Directional recording of swell from distant storms", Phil. Trans. R. Soc. A 255, 505-584. Swell from Indian Ocean storms, 10,000+ km away, dated to the day. **must**
- **Snodgrass, F. E., Groves, G. W., Hasselmann, K. F., Miller, G. R., Munk, W. H. & Powers, W. H. 1966**, "Propagation of ocean swell across the Pacific", Phil. Trans. R. Soc. A 259, 431-497. Transect from New Zealand to Alaska; attenuation and dispersion of a single storm's swell. **must** (the paper's "layers" language is closest to this)
- **Collard, F., Ardhuin, F. & Chapron, B. 2009**, "Monitoring and analysis of ocean swell fields from space: new methods for routine observations", J. Geophys. Res. 114, C07023, doi:10.1029/2008JC005215, arXiv:0812.2318. SAR "fireworks" back-propagation to storm source in space and time. should
- **Longuet-Higgins, M. S. 1963**, "The generation of capillary waves by steep gravity waves", J. Fluid Mech. 16, 138-159. Parasitic capillaries on short gravity waves (analogue of small-scale features riding on the swell). nice
- **Gerling, T. W. 1992**, "Partitioning sequences and arrays of directional ocean wave spectra into component wave systems", J. Atmos. Ocean. Tech. 9, 444-458; **Hanson, J. L. & Phillips, O. M. 2001**, "Automated analysis of ocean surface directional wave spectra", J. Atmos. Ocean. Tech. 18, 277-293. Spectral partitioning into wind sea and multiple swell systems, each tracked to a source. should (if the paper separates "storms")

## 2. "When did the storm start" in cosmology

- **Lemaître, G. 1927**, Ann. Soc. Sci. Bruxelles A47, 49 (transl. MNRAS 91, 483, 1931); **Lemaître 1931**, "The beginning of the world from the point of view of quantum theory", Nature 127, 706. Expansion age; primeval atom. **must**
- **Hubble, E. 1929**, PNAS 15, 168-173. The Hubble time as the first age estimate (~2 Gyr, younger than the Earth: the first age problem). **must**
- **Sandage, A. 1970**, "Cosmology: a search for two numbers", Physics Today 23(2), 34. should
- **Krauss, L. M. & Chaboyer, B. 2003**, "Age estimates of globular clusters in the Milky Way: constraints on cosmology", Science 299, 65-69. 1990s age problem and its resolution by Lambda. **must**
- **Jimenez, R. & Loeb, A. 2002**, "Constraining cosmological parameters based on relative galaxy ages", ApJ 573, 37, astro-ph/0106145; **Moresco, M. et al. 2012**, JCAP 08, 006, arXiv:1201.3609; **Moresco et al. 2022**, "Unveiling the universe with emerging cosmological probes", Living Rev. Relativ. 25, 6, arXiv:2201.07241. Cosmic chronometers: H(z) with no CMB, no ruler. **must** (a genuinely independent clock)
- **Cayrel, R. et al. 2001**, "Measurement of stellar age from uranium decay", Nature 409, 691-692; **Hill, V. et al. 2002**, A&A 387, 560, astro-ph/0203462. Nucleocosmochronology: U/Th in CS 31082-001, 12.5 +- 3 Gyr. should
- **Hansen, B. M. S. et al. 2002**, ApJL 574, L155, astro-ph/0205087; **Hansen et al. 2004**, ApJS 155, 551. White-dwarf cooling age of M4, 12.7 +- 0.7 Gyr. should
- **Bond, H. E. et al. 2013**, "HD 140283: a star in the solar neighborhood that formed shortly after the Big Bang", ApJL 765, L12, arXiv:1302.3180. should
- **Planck Collaboration 2020 (VI)**, A&A 641, A6, arXiv:1807.06209: t0 = 13.797 +- 0.023 Gyr. **must**

## 3. Ages from large-scale structure alone

- **Eisenstein, D. J. & Hu, W. 1998**, "Baryonic features in the matter transfer function", ApJ 496, 605, astro-ph/9709112. Sound horizon r_d as standard ruler; the fitting formulae. **must**
- **Eisenstein, D. J. et al. 2005**, "Detection of the baryon acoustic peak in the large-scale correlation function of SDSS luminous red galaxies", ApJ 633, 560, astro-ph/0501171; Cole, S. et al. 2005 (2dFGRS), MNRAS 362, 505. Detection. **must**
- **Aubourg, E. et al. 2015**, "Cosmological implications of baryon acoustic oscillation measurements", PRD 92, 123516, arXiv:1411.1074. BAO + BBN prior on omega_b gives H0 (67.3 +- 1.1) with no CMB anisotropy data. **must; direct anticipation of "age from the waves alone"** (an age follows immediately from their H0, Omega_m).
- **Schoneberg, N., Lesgourgues, J. & Hooper, D. C. 2019**, "The BAO+BBN take on the Hubble tension", JCAP 10, 029, arXiv:1907.11594; Schoneberg, Abellan et al. 2022, "BAO+BBN revisited", JCAP 11, 039, arXiv:2209.14330. Background history "independently of both CMB maps and supernovae". **must**
- **Ivanov, M. M., Simonovic, M. & Zaldarriaga, M. 2020**, "Cosmological parameters from the BOSS galaxy power spectrum", JCAP 05, 042, arXiv:1909.05277; **d'Amico, G. et al. 2020**, JCAP 05, 005, arXiv:1909.05271. First full-shape EFT analyses; H0 from P(k) with only a BBN prior. **must**
- **Philcox, O. H. E., Ivanov, M. M., Simonovic, M. & Zaldarriaga, M. 2020**, "Combining full-shape and BAO analyses of galaxy power spectra: a 1.6% CMB-independent constraint on H0", JCAP 05, 032, arXiv:2002.04035. **must; direct anticipation.** **Philcox, Farren, Sherwin et al. 2022**, "Determining the Hubble constant without the sound horizon", PRD 106, 063530, arXiv:2204.02984 (uses the equality scale instead of r_d: the "swell" not the "fossil sound"). **must**
- **DESI Collaboration 2024 (VII)**, "Cosmological constraints from the full-shape modeling of clustering measurements", arXiv:2411.12022. Full-shape + BBN gives H0 and Omega_m; no explicit age quoted, but derivable. should
- No paper found that states "age of the universe from LSS alone" as a headline number; the closest are the H0 papers above plus cosmic chronometers. Claiming novelty for the *framing* is defensible; for the *measurement* it is not.

## 4. Reading epochs from spectral features

- **Silk, J. 1968**, "Cosmic black-body radiation and galaxy formation", ApJ 151, 459. Damping scale. **must**
- **Peebles, P. J. E. & Yu, J. T. 1970**, "Primeval adiabatic perturbation in an expanding universe", ApJ 162, 815; **Sunyaev & Zeldovich 1970**, Ap&SS 7, 3. Acoustic oscillations in the matter spectrum. **must**
- **Hu, W. & Sugiyama, N. 1996**, "Small-scale cosmological perturbations: an analytic approach", ApJ 471, 542, astro-ph/9510117. Drag epoch z_d, its distinction from z_*. **must**
- **Peacock, J. A. & Dodds, S. J. 1994**, MNRAS 267, 1020; **Percival, W. J. et al. 2001**, "The 2dF Galaxy Redshift Survey: the power spectrum and the matter content of the Universe", MNRAS 327, 1297, astro-ph/0105252. Equality scale from the turnover (Gamma = Omega_m h). **must**
- **Bashinsky, S. & Seljak, U. 2004**, "Signatures of relativistic neutrinos in CMB anisotropy and matter clustering", PRD 69, 083002, astro-ph/0310198; **Baumann, D., Green, D., Meyers, J. & Wallisch, B. 2016**, "Phases of new physics in the CMB", JCAP 01, 007, arXiv:1508.06342. Neutrino phase shift (the "refraction"). **must**
- **Baumann, D., Beutler, F., Flauger, R., Green, D., Slosar, A., Vargas-Magana, M., Wallisch, B. & Yeche, C. 2019**, "First constraint on the neutrino-induced phase shift in the spectrum of baryon acoustic oscillations", Nature Physics 15, 465-469, arXiv:1803.10741. **must; direct anticipation of the refraction reading**.
- **Planck 2018 X** (inflation), A&A 641, A10, arXiv:1807.06211; **Beutler, F. et al. 2019**, "Primordial features from linear to nonlinear scales", PRR 1, 033209, arXiv:1906.08758; **Ballardini, M. et al. 2020**, JCAP 10, 048, arXiv:1912.12499. Feature searches in P(k). should
- Stratigraphy/timeline framing: not found explicitly; nearest is Hu & Dodelson 2002, ARA&A 40, 171. nice

## 5. The sea/swell analogy itself

- **Eisenstein's public pages** (deisenstein.scholars.harvard.edu, "Baryon Acoustic Oscillations") and Eisenstein, Seo & White 2007, ApJ 664, 660 (the "sound wave" picture): waves "frozen" into the matter at decoupling. should
- **Hu, W.**, "Sound waves" pages (background.uchicago.edu); Hu & White 2004, "The cosmic symphony", Sci. Am. 290, 44. should
- "Fossil" usage: Peebles & Yu 1970 do not use it; it is common in later BAO reviews ("fossil imprint", Weinberg et al. 2013, Phys. Rep. 530, 87, arXiv:1201.2434). nice
- No prior use of the ocean-swell dispersion clock (Munk/Barber-Ursell) as an analogy for dating the Big Bang was found. The analogy appears original; the measurement it motivates is not.

## Anticipations to acknowledge

Aubourg 2015 and Philcox 2020 (CMB-independent H0, hence age, from the BAO/full shape with BBN); Philcox 2022 (age from the equality scale, i.e. the swell alone); Baumann 2019 (phase shift as refraction); cosmic chronometers as the wholly independent clock.
