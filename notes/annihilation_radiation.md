# Could the kernel's radiation be relic/anti-relic annihilation? (2026-09-05, contact_annihilation_long.py)
Mobile relics on a jammed lattice with full reshuffle between rounds, exact CP: survivors 0.19 (1 round), 1.1e-2 (30),
3.3e-3 (100), 8.7e-4 (400); late-time law survivors ~ rounds^-0.98 (two-body kinetics with mixing). Reaching the
survivor fraction Sec 5 needs: 1e-13 -> 5e12 reshuffles; 1e-28 (T_conv = T_P, which annihilation gives) -> 1e28.
A 1e-3 relic excess floors the survivors at 2e-3 (asymmetric case). Reshuffles available: one per recompression wave
passage through the jammed interior, O(10-100) in the toys (a static jammed pack is immobile: 17.5% floor).
Verdict: annihilation removes the 93-generation evaporation delay and the cross-section (contact is geometric and
continuous in the jammed state) and leaves cold survivors (no hot-DM problem), but exchanges the 28-order
cross-section gap for an 11-26-order reshuffle gap. Requires relic/anti-relic pairs (CPT conjugate remnants) and
still imports baryogenesis (4b). Survivors are same-sign clusters, i.e. clumps, which under the remnant clock
evaporate in N^3 t_P to one Planck-mass remnant each: cluster count, not relic count, is the floor.
